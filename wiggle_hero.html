The framework decomposes judge instability

Every perturbation is applied to the same items, judges, and grading scales — so mechanical, conformity, and persuadability effects become directly comparable for the first time.

The graduated pressure ladder

Seven rungs, escalating conviction tests

L0 is the judge left alone. Each rung above applies a stronger, more socially-loaded challenge — [opposite] is always the verdict opposing the judge’s own L0 baseline.

Pressure that works is net-corrupting.

Across 60 ground-truthed conditions, a successful challenge moves the verdict away from the label 56–70% of the time. Only 3 of 60 conditions show a significantly corrective effect.

Binary and Likert scales even flip in opposite directions on the same items — so the grading scale you pick also chooses which kind of error pressure will introduce.
